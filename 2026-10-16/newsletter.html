What's New
 
Annie stops answering every message from your on-call bot
Featured
Registering a monitoring bot or a teammate with /register_annie_on_call or @Annie register now sets Annie up for new alerts and incidents only. Resolved, acknowledged, reassigned and severity-change messages, plus digests, deploy notices and chat, no longer start an investigation. Rules you already edited are never overwritten, and registering twice does not create duplicates.
/drop_annie_on_call removes the sender and the unedited default rules it generated, and keeps the ones you customized.
Read the Slack guide
Slack investigations read the thread they started in
When Annie answers in Slack, she now knows the channel and thread the message came from. She can read what responders already posted there, so an investigation starts from what your team has found instead of from zero.
SEE HOW SLACK ALERTS BECOME INVESTIGATIONS
Failed Kubernetes pods now carry their exit code
Pods that fail without restarting, such as Job pods and other run-to-completion workloads, used to show up as Failed with no detail. They now carry the container's termination reason and exit code in your graph, for example Error with exit code 1. Annie reads the real failure signal when you ask about a failed Job.

Incoming
Annie's memory, rebuilt from scratch
A daily job now reads each project's conversations and keeps only durable infrastructure facts: how things are wired, who owns what, naming and retention. Every entry keeps its source and every change is logged with a reason. It runs in shadow today, writing only, and nothing is injected into Annie's answers yet. Next step is reviewing the first real runs, then deciding how Annie reads it, as hints with their sources rather than as facts.
